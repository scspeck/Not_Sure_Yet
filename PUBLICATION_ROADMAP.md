# Publication roadmap

## 1. Release candidate
Complete provenance, controlled validation, browser/performance tests, resolve failures, and freeze behavior.

## 2. Public v1.0.0
Clean repository, validate citation metadata, publish tagged release, verify live deployment, and archive the release.

## 3. Demonstrated research use
Document real workflows and collaborative/external use while maintaining public issues, commits, and releases.

## 4. Software paper
Complete state-of-the-field comparison, demonstrated impact, software design, references, acknowledgements, and AI disclosure.

For JOSS specifically, current screening guidance requires at least six months of public development history and demonstrated research impact. Technical completion alone is not sufficient.
